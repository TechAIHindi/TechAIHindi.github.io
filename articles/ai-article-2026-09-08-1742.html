Online Fraud Aur Cyber Crime Se Kaise Bachain: Indian Users Ke Liye Zaroori Safety Guide

Aaj ke samay mein hamare lagbhag sabhi kaam mobile phone aur internet se jude hue hain. Chahe Google Pay ya PhonePe se paise bhejna ho, electricity bill bharna ho, online shopping karni ho ya WhatsApp par dosto se baat karni ho, hamari rozana ki zindagi digital technology par nirbhar hai. Lekin is badhte digital upyog ke saath ek badi chunauti bhi samne aayi hai—Cyber Fraud aur Online Scams.

Pichle kuch samay mein Bharat mein naye-naye tarike ke cyber crimes dekhne ko mile hain. Digital arrest ke naam par darana, fake APK files bhejkar phone hack karna aur part-time job ke naam par paise thagna ab aam baat ho chuki hai. Lekin achhi baat yeh hai ki thodi si savdhani aur sahi jankari se aap apne aur apne parivar ke hard-earned money aur personal data ko bilkul surakshit rakh sakte hain.

Is comprehensive guide mein hum baat karenge ki aaj kal Bharat mein kaun-kaun se online scams chal rahe hain aur aap unse kaise bach sakte hain.

Aaj Kal Bharat Mein Hone Wale 4 Sabse Khatarnak Online Scams

Cyber criminals logon ki bholabhalahat aur darr ka fayda uthate hain. Agar aapko pehle se pata hoga ki frauds kaise hote hain, toh aap kabhi inka shikar nahi banenge.

1. Digital Arrest Scam (नकली पुलिस या CBI कॉल)

Yeh aaj kal ka sabse bada aur darr paida karne wala scam hai. Ismein fraud karne wale aapko WhatsApp video call ya phone call karte hain aur kehne lagte hain ki aapke naam par koi illegal parcel pakda gaya hai, jismein drug ya koi galat samaan hai. Ya fir yeh kehte hain ki aapka mobile number kisi crime mein use hua hai.

Yeh log fake police uniform ya CBI officer ban kar baat karte hain aur aapko video call par bane rehne ko kehte hain, jise yeh 'Digital Arrest' kehte hain. Darr dikhakar yeh aapse mamla rafa-dafa karne ke naam par lakhon rupaye bank account mein transfer karwa lete hain.

Dhyan dein: Bharatiya kanoon mein 'Digital Arrest' naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti. Koi bhi police ya sarkari agency video call par kisi ko arrest nahi karti aur na hi paise maangti hai.

2. Fake APK File Scam (WhatsApp Par Dangerous Apps)

Aaj kal WhatsApp aur SMS par tarah-tarah ke message aate hain, jaise—"Aapka Bijli Bill Update Nahi Hua Hai, Is App Ko Download Karein" ya "Wedding Card PDF View Karein" ya "PM Yojna Ke Tehat Free Recharge Payein".

In messages ke saath ek .apk file attach hoti hai. Jaise hi aap is file par click karke install karte hain, aapke phone mein ek malware (virus) install ho jata hai. Yeh malware aapke phone ke SMS, OTP aur banking apps ka control hackers ko de deta hai, jisse aapke account se paise kat jate hain.

3. Bank Account Block Aur Utility Bill Warning Scam

Is scam mein aapko ek urjeit SMS ya WhatsApp message milta hai jismein likha hota hai ki aapka bank account, SIM card ya PAN card block kar diya gaya hai. SMS mein ek link diya hota hai aur turant update karne ko kaha jata hai. Link par click karte hi ek fake website khulti hai jo bilkul asli bank jaisi lagti hai. Jaise hi aap wahan apna user ID, password ya OTP dalte hain, aapki details chori ho jaati hain.

4. Part-Time Job Aur Telegram Rating Scam

Is scam mein aapko Work-From-Home ka offer diya jata hai. Keha jata hai ki aapko YouTube videos like karne hain ya Google par hotels ko rating deni hai aur aapko roz 2000 se 5000 rupaye milenge. Shuru mein yeh aapko 200-500 rupaye bhejkar vishwas jit-te hain. Uske baad yeh aapko Telegram group mein add karke 'VIP Task' ke naam par paise invest karne ko kehte hain. Ek baar paise lagane ke baad yeh aapke paise wapas nahi dete.

Cyber Fraud Se Bachne Ke 7 Practical Steps

Online duniya mein surakshit rehne ke liye aapko kisi heavy technical knowledge ki zaroorat nahi hai. Bas in 7 aasan niyam ka palan karein:

  1. Kabhie Bhi Anjaan APK Files Download Na Karein: Kisi bhi WhatsApp group ya SMS se aaye hue .apk format file ko install na karein. Koi bhi app hamesha official Google Play Store ya Apple App Store se hi download karein.
  2. Video Call Par Darr Se Bachein: Agar koi aapko call karke police, CBI, ED ya Customs officer bata kar draye, toh turant phone kaat dein. Apne nazdeeki police station mein jakar baat karein ya official customer care par call karein.
  3. OTP, PIN Aur Password Kisi Ko Na Batayein: Aapka Bank OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN aur Banking Password bilkul secret cheezein hain. Bank ka koi bhi karmchari ya police officer kabhi bhi aapse OTP ya PIN nahi maangta.
  4. UPI QR Code Ka Sahi Niyam Samjhein: Hamesha yaad rakhein ki Paise lene ke liye UPI PIN daalne ki zaroorat nahi hoti. UPI PIN sirf tabhi daala jata hai jab aap kisi ko paise bhej rahe hote hain. Agar koi keh raha hai ki paise lene ke liye QR code scan karo ya PIN daalo, toh woh fraud hai.
  5. Unknown Links Par Click Na Karein: SMS ya WhatsApp par aaye kisi bhi suspicious link (jaise bit.ly, free gift links, bill pay links) par click na karein. Bank ki official website hamesha khud browser mein type karke kholein.
  6. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) On Karein: Apne WhatsApp, Gmail, Facebook aur Banking apps par Two-Factor Authentication hamesha active rakhein. Isse agar kisi ko aapka password pata bhi chal jaye, tab bhi woh aapka account login nahi kar payega.
  7. App Permissions Hamesha Check Karein: Jab bhi aap koi naya app install karein, toh yeh zaroor dekhein ki app kya-kya permission maang raha hai. Kisi calculator ya photo editing app ko aapke SMS ya Contacts ki permission dene ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

Agar Aapke Saath Fraud Ho Jaye Toh Turant Kya Karein?

Agar kisi wajah se aap kisi online scam ka shikar ho jate hain ya aapke account se galat tarike se paise kat jate hain, toh ghabrane ki bajaye turant ye steps utayein:

  • 1. Cyber Crime National Helpline (1930) Par Call Karein: Bharat sarkar ne cyber fraud se bachav ke liye Toll-Free Number 1930 jari kiya hai. Incident hone ke 1 se 2 ghante ke andar (Golden Hour) call karne par aapke paise fraudster ke account mein freeze kar diye jate hain aur aapko paise wapas milne ke chances bahut badh jate hain.
  • 2. Online Complaint Register Karein: Sarkar ki official website cybercrime.gov.in par ja kar apni shikayat darj karein. Yahan transaction details aur screenshot attach karein.
  • 3. Apne Bank Ko Inform Karein: Immediately apne bank ke customer care par call karke apna debit/credit card block karwayein aur net banking ko temporary freeze karwayein.
  • 4. Local Cyber Police Station Jayein: Apne nazdeeki police station ke cyber cell mein written complaint dein.

APK File Kya Hoti Hai aur Yeh Khatarnak Kyun Ho Sakti Hai?

Jab aap Google Play Store se koi app download karte hain, toh background me ek file download hoti hai jise APK (Android Package Kit) kaha jata hai. Yeh Android operating system ke liye kisi app ka installation package hota hai, bilkul waise hi jaise Windows PC ke liye `.exe` file hoti hai.

Google Play Store par aane wali sabhi apps ko Google ke security checks aur Google Play Protect se guzarna padta hai. Lekin jab aap Play Store ke bahar kisi anjaan website, WhatsApp group ya Telegram channel se koi `.apk` file download karke install karte hain, toh aap Android ke basic security barrier ko bypass kar dete hain.

Khatarnak ya Malicious APK files ke andar chupa hua virus (trojan) aapke phone me install hote hi ye sab kar sakta hai:

  • SMS Read Permission: Aapke phone par aane wale sabhi Bank OTPs ko secretly hacker ke server par bhej dena.
  • Accessibility Permission: Aapke screen par typed passwords aur PIN numbers ko record kar lena.
  • Contact & Photos Access: Aapki personal photos aur contacts ko steal karke blackmail karna.
  • Remote Control: Aapke phone ko bina aapki jankari ke background me operate karna.

Bharat me Sabse Zyada Hone Wale 4 APK Scams

Cyber criminals alag-alag tarike se logon ko jhaanse me lete hain. Bharat me filhaal niche diye gaye scams sabse zyada dekhe ja rahe hain:

1. Electricity Bill aur Water Bill Fraud

User ko ek urgent SMS bheja jata hai ki unka bijli ka bill update nahi hua hai aur aaj raat supply kaat di jayegi. SMS me ek link hoti hai jise click karne par ek APK file download hoti hai. Yeh app bill pay karne ke naam par banking details aur OTP chura leti hai.

2. Fake Courier & Speed Post Tracking Scam

India Post, Blue Dart ya Delhivery ke naam se SMS bheja jata hai ki aapka parcel address galat hone ki wajah se ruka hua hai. App download karke ₹5 ya ₹10 ka re-delivery charge dene ko kaha jata hai. Jaise hi user payment karta hai, card details compromised ho jati hain.

3. Bank KYC Update Scam

SBI, HDFC ya kisi anya bank ke naam se nakli app ki link bheji jati hai. Yeh claim kiya jata hai ki KYC update na hone par account block ho jayega. User jaise hi iss app me login credential daalta hai, uske account se paise nikal liye jaate hain.

4. Part-time Job aur Earn Money Apps

Ghar baithe 2000-5000 rupaye rozana kamane ka lalach dekar Telegram ya WhatsApp par ek APK file bheji jati hai. Yeh apps shuru me chote tasks ke paise deti hain aur baad me bada investment karwa kar fraud karti hain.

Zaroori Baat: Koi bhi sarkari vibhag, bank ya reputed courier company kabhi bhi aapko WhatsApp par kisi `.apk` file ko download karne ke liye nahi kehti.

Fake Apps aur Malicious APK ko Pehchanne ke 5 Aasan Tarike

Apne smartphone ko secure rakhne ke liye aapko thodi si alert rehne ki zaroorat hai. Kisi bhi app ko install karne se pehle in baaton ka khayal rakhein:

  1. Source ki Jaanch Karein: App humesha official Google Play Store se hi download karein. Kisi website link, SMS ya chat app se mili file ko install na karein.
  2. Developer ki Jankari Check Karein: Play Store par app download karne se pehle developer ka naam dhyaan se padhein. Fake apps aksar kisi popular company ke naam se milti-julti hoti hain (jaise PhonePe ki jagah PhonePay ya SBI ki jagah SBI-Online).
  3. Downloads Count aur Reviews Padhein: Kisi genuine banking ya official app ke lakho-caroro downloads hote hain. Agar kisi app ke downloads bahut kam hain ya reviews me logon ne fraud ki shikayat ki hai, toh usse door rahein.
  4. Unnecessary Permissions par Nazarr Rakhein: Agar koi simple Calculator ya Torch app aapke Contacts, SMS, Location ya Call Logs ki permission maange, toh turant alert ho jayein.
  5. Spelling aur Grammar ki Galti: Fraudulent apps aur unki messaging me aksar spelling aur sentence formatting me galti hoti hai.

Android Smartphone me Safety Settings Kaise On Karein?

Aapke Android phone me kuch aisi settings hoti hain jo aapko aise khatron se bacha sakti hain. Inhe abhi check karke enable karein:

Step 1: "Install Unknown Apps" ko Disable Karein

Apne phone ki Settings > Apps > Special App Access > Install Unknown Apps me jayein. Yahan Chrome, WhatsApp aur File Manager jaise apps ke samne "Allow from this source" ko OFF (Disable) kar dein. Isse bina aapki permission ke koi bhi APK file install nahi ho sakegi.

Step 2: Google Play Protect ko Active Rakhein

Google Play Store open karein, profile icon par tap karein aur Play Protect option par jayein. Yahan settings icon par click karke "Scan apps with Play Protect" aur "Improve harmful app detection" dono options ko ON rakhein.

Play Protect aapke phone me majood sabhi apps ko time-to-time scan karta rehta hai aur kisi bhi suspicious activity par aapko warning deta hai.

Agar Galti se Malicious APK Install Ho Jaye Toh Kya Karein?

Agar aapne galti se koi suspicious APK file install kar li hai, toh ghabrane ki bajaye turant ye steps follow karein:

  • Internet Disconnect Karein: Sabse pehle apne phone ka Mobile Data aur Wi-Fi turant OFF kar dein taaki app hacker ke server ko data na bhej sake.
  • App ko Uninstall Karein: Settings > Apps me jaakar us app ko khojein aur Uninstall karein. Agar app uninstall nahi ho rahi hai, toh phone ko Safe Mode me restart karke uninstall karein.
  • Permissions Revoke Karein: Phone ki Settings me jaakar Accessibility Services check karein ki kisi unknown app ko access toh nahi mila hai.
  • Passwords Change Karein: Kisi doosre secure device ka istemal karke apne net banking, UPI apps aur email accounts ke passwords change karein.
  • Bank ko Inform Karein: Agar aapko lagta hai ki aapki banking details leak hui hain, toh turant apne bank ke customer care par call karke apna card/account temporarily block karwayein.

Cyber Fraud Hone Par Shikayat Kahan Karein?

Agar aap kisi online fraud ya cyber crime ka shikar ho jate hain, toh bina samay gawaye Bharat Sarkar ke official helpline numbers aur portal par complaint register karein:

Aap National Cyber Crime Helpline Number: 1930 par call kar sakte hain ya sarkar ki official website cybercrime.gov.in par apni complaint online dakhil kar sakte hain. Fraud hone ke shuruaati 2 ghante (Golden Hours) me complaint darj karwane par paise wapas milne ki sambhavna sabse zyada hoti hai.
